The first release of the Qt 6 line. Highlights:

- Analog needle instrument and LCD display panel
- Recorder on Qt Charts with thresholds, integration curve, CSV export
- Calculated values across instances and a built-in virtual meter
- qtdmm-bridge: meters on a Raspberry Pi, QtDMM at the desk
- 127 supported meters, USB-HID cables, Windows installer

Download for Windows or build from source — see the [download page](download.html).
