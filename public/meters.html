?
Not yet confirmed — 60 models were added from chip datasheets and protocol documentation and have not been tried with QtDMM. Got one? Tell us whether it works.
mod
Hardware modification needed — sold without an interface; the chip can talk, but a wire or IR LED has to be soldered in first:
  • Generic DTM0660 4000 count, DTM0660 6000 count, DTM0660 8000 count: the DTM0660 chip has a UART, but meters built on it (Victor VC921 and similar) ship without an interface: an IR LED with a 100 Ω resistor goes on the chip's TX pin and the RS232 bit in the 24C02 EEPROM has to be set - eevblog: hacking the Victor VC-921. PeakTech 3415 and Velleman DVM4100 use the same chip but come with a cable and need no change.
  • Vichy VC99: the FS9922-DMM4 chip sends the display at 2400 baud on an unused pin; a wire or IR LED has to be soldered to it and REL held to start the output - Hackaday, David Pilling.
